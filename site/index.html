RDPNO {{VERSION}} · experimental

Roland's SA pianos on the M-VAVE FM-1: the MKS-20's pianos and e-pianos and the Rhodes MK-80, played by an emulation of their CPU-B board (its CPU and the SA sound chip, cycle for cycle), after rdpiano by Giulio Zausa. With the MKS-20's equalizer, chorus (Space-D or BBD) and tremolo, up to 8 notes at once over both of the FM-1's cores.

The sounds are Roland's ROMs, and they are not part of RDPNO: you load your own dumps into the FM-1 from this site. One set at a time: MKS-20 A (Piano 1–3), MKS-20 B (Harpsichord, Clavi, Vibraphone, E-Piano 1, 2) or MK-80 (8 tones).

1 · InstallRDPNO onto the FM-1 over USB, and your ROMs with it. 2 · Load the ROMsanother set, or again after something went wrong. 3 · Editvoice, equalizer, tremolo, chorus from the browser, live; your setups. The screenswhat HOME, FX and ENV show and do.

Chrome or Edge (Web MIDI), the FM-1 connected by USB. Back to M-VAVE's firmware at any time: the installer's "Back to the stock firmware", or M-VAVE's own updater. Installing firmware is at your own risk; if the FM-1 no longer starts, hold OCT- while switching it on (the USB rescue).

The firmware file itself: {{PKG}} (no Roland data in it). Source, and how to build it yourself: github.com/R-Jeb/rdpno-fm1.
