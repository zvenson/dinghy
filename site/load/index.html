Load the ROMs

RDPNO plays Roland's own sound ROMs, and they are not part of it: this page makes a ROM pack from your own dumps and puts it into the FM-1's flash. One set at a time; loading another replaces it. A firmware update clears it (the installer loads it again in the same go). Chrome or Edge, the FM-1 connected by USB and running RDPNO.

1 · Connect

Close other pages and apps that use the FM-1's MIDI first.

2 · Your ROM files

Choose the files, all at once if you like: the page takes the ones a set needs, recognised by their content (or by the names rdpiano gives them). They are read here, in the browser.

Each set needs five files: three sound ROMs, one parameter ROM, and the program ROM of the CPU-B board (RD200_B.bin), which every set needs. The list below shows what the chosen set needs and ticks off what you have chosen; the extension does not matter (.bin, .rom).

3 · Load

About a minute or two. The FM-1 is silent meanwhile, shows LOADING THE ROMS, and restarts with them at the end. Don't unplug it until this page says done.

Your last pack is kept in this browser (only here), so the installer can load it again after an update.
